# REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL amending Regulation (EU) 2018/1727 as regards the extension of the timeframe for the establishment of the Eurojust case management system

## DIGITAL DIMENSIONS

This proposal provides for the extension of the timeframe for the establishment of the new Eurojust case management system in order to allow Eurojust to test and ensure the operationality and interoperability of this new system, as well as to allow the relevant data migration. Although the proposal amends the Regulation (EU) 2018/1727, which is part of the digitalisation of justice package, it does not introduce any new requirements of digital relevance. Consequently, the ‘digital by default’ principle does not apply. While the establishment of the “new” CMS is clearly of high digital relevance, it falls outside the scope of this digital statement.
